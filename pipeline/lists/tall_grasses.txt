# 12 lush, somewhat tall grasses (Poaceae), chosen by hand for variety of plume and leaf form
Cenchrus alopecuroides        # fountain grass (formerly Pennisetum alopecuroides): arching leaves, bottlebrush plumes
Miscanthus sinensis          # Chinese silver grass: tall arching clump, silvery plumes
Panicum virgatum             # switchgrass: upright, airy open panicles
Andropogon gerardii          # big bluestem: tall prairie grass, three-branched "turkey foot" seed heads
Sorghastrum nutans           # Indiangrass: tall, golden feathery panicles
Cortaderia selloana          # pampas grass: very tall tussock, large white plumes
Phragmites australis         # common reed: tall stands, feathery purple-brown plumes
Arundo donax                 # giant reed: tall cane-like stems, broad leaves
Phalaris arundinacea         # reed canary grass: lush, dense, tall stands
Chasmanthium latifolium      # northern sea oats: flat dangling oat-like spikelets
Molinia caerulea             # purple moor grass: tufts with tall airy panicles
Tripsacum dactyloides        # eastern gamagrass: lush broad-leaved bunchgrass
